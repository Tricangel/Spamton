# Spamton

Adds packages/cardboard boxes, Parcels, Parcel shelves, and Letters

## Packages

Small storage (9 slots) that can be picked up when taped. Has a chance of breaking if retaped (taped, untaped, and taped again)

### Stamps
Way to identify and distinguish packages (or just make them cute :D) Gives you a stamp of a player when clicked on a player or player head

## Letter
Worse but cheaper version of a book and quill, has a maximum of three pages

## Parcel
Stores a writable item (letter or book and quill) along with a gift item (any non-writable)

### Parcel Shelf
Stores up to 9 parcels
